        10 Password Security Rules

        1. Use unique passwords for different accounts.
        2. Prefer longer passwords or randomly selected passphrases.
        3. Avoid predictable personal information.
        4. Avoid common passwords and keyboard walks.
        5. Do not reuse passwords across services.
        6. Use a password manager for unique credentials.
        7. Enable MFA where available.
        8. Never share passwords with other people or support agents.
        9. Be cautious of phishing pages and suspicious login requests.
        10. Change credentials when compromise is suspected or confirmed.

        Educational defensive-security project. Strength scoring is project-defined and not a universal security standard.
